# Bundled Bible texts

These texts are bundled locally so the Bible reader works without an internet connection.

- Berean Standard Bible (BSB): dedicated to the public domain on April 30, 2023. Source and terms: https://berean.bible/terms.htm
- World English Bible (WEB), Protestant 66-book edition: public domain. The World English Bible name identifies an unmodified copy of the translation. Source and terms: https://worldenglish.bible/
- King James Version (KJV), standardized 1769 text, canonical 66 books: public domain outside the United Kingdom. In the United Kingdom, printing and publication are governed by the Crown's letters patent. Source: https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/

Verse text was obtained from eBible.org's official developer archives. Application source-code licensing does not alter the status of these Bible texts.
